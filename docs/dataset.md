# Dataset background

## Source microscopy

The project starts from seven aligned high-resolution microscopy images of the same sandstone thin-section:

- 1 Plane-Polarized Light image (`PPL`)
- 6 Cross-Polarized Light images (`PPX1` to `PPX6`) captured at 15° angle increments between 0° and 75°

In this codebase, `PPX` refers to cross-polarized light (`XPL`).

Two subsections of the thin-section are used for the thesis experiments:

- A larger section for tuning, training, validation, and U-Net postprocessing selection.
- A smaller held-out section for final testing.

## Labels (QGIS)

Initial labels were generated by running SAM2 on the PPL image using sliding-window inference. Mask polygons were imported in QGIS, manually corrected and refined, quality-checked (invalid geometries, holes, fully-contained features removed), then smoothed and buffered by 5 px.

QGIS processing introduced overlapping polygons. A custom **overlap splitting** step produces strictly non-overlapping grain masks where previously overlapping polygons touch at a shared boundary.

The algorithm:

1. Identifies connected components of overlapping polygons using a spatial index and bounding-box intersection graph.
2. For each overlapping pair, computes the exact intersection polygon.
3. Calculates a topological centerline that splits the overlap in two halves using Voronoi polygons (`pygeoops`).
4. Smooths the centerline (Taubin and Chaikin) to remove Voronoi-originated zigzags.
5. Snaps centerline endpoints to the outer boundaries of the intersecting grains.
6. Splits the overlap along the smoothed centerline.
7. Assigns halves to the original adjacent polygons using a ray-cast heuristic from the centerline midpoint to exclusive areas of each polygon.

Corrected polygons and aligned microscopy images are cropped to the selected train and test sections.

## Semantic classes (U-Net)

For U-Net, polygons are rasterized into three semantic classes:

- background
- grain interior
- grain boundary (3 px band around each grain)

## Input variants

Two composite variants are derived from per-channel TIFFs:

- `PPLPPXblend`: single composite from PPL and PPX images (screen blending).
- `PPL+PPXblend`: two-input variant using PPL plus a screen-blended PPX composite.

Stacked YOLO mosaics (`train_PPL+PPXblend.tif`, `train_PPL+AllPPX.tif`, etc.) are built during preprocessing; U-Net whole manifests list **per-channel** TIFFs only. See [`reference/scratch-layout.md`](reference/scratch-layout.md).

## Train/validation split and patches

To process the large training section:

1. **Spatial tiling:** The training section is divided into 4096×4096 spatial tiles.
2. **Coverage stratification:** Grain coverage is computed per tile. Tiles with less than 10% grain coverage go strictly to training. Remaining eligible tiles are binned by coverage and split with stratified sampling (80% train, 20% validation).
3. **Patch extraction:** Selected tiles are cropped into 1024×1024 patches. Training tiles use 50% overlap; validation and test tiles use 0% overlap (non-overlapping grid). YOLO receives instance polygon labels; U-Net receives raster semantic masks.

Preprocessing runbook: [`runbooks/preprocessing.md`](runbooks/preprocessing.md).
